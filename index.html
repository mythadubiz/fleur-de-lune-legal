Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Fleur de Lune is designed to respect your privacy. We do not sell your data, and the game contains no advertising.

What we collect

  • Nothing by default. Your game progress, story, garden and settings are stored on your device (and, if you enable it, in your own private iCloud via Apple CloudKit).
  • Anonymous analytics — only with your consent. If (and only if) you enable “Anonymous statistics”, the app sends anonymous, aggregated usage events (e.g. chapter started/completed) through Google Firebase Analytics to help us improve the game. It is off by default, you are asked once, and you can change it any time in Settings. These events do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA), do not identify you, and are never used for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Handled by Apple

  • In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We never see or store your payment details.
  • iCloud save (if enabled) is stored in your private iCloud account under Apple's privacy policy.

Children

The game is rated 12+ (themes of mystery, loss and suggested — never graphic — events). It contains no ads and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Turn anonymous statistics on or off at any time in Settings.
  • Deleting the app removes all locally stored data from your device.
  • Contact us at mythadubiz@gmail.com for any privacy request.
We may update this policy as the game evolves. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a new date.
